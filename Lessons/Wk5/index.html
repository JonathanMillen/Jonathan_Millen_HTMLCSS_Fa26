Artisan Pizza Dough Recipe

This is our favorite pizza dough recipe! It is easy to make and yields a delicious, chewy crust. The dough can be made ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months. Enjoy!

Ingredients for Artisan Pizza Dough

To create this incredible artisan pizza dough, you will need the following ingredients:
  • 500g(3 ¾ + 2 tbsp) all-purpose flour
  • 10g (1 ½ tsp) fine salt
  • 1g (¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 375g (1 ½ cup + 2 tbsp) warm water

Equipment Needed

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Kitchen scale (optional but recommended)
  • Plastic wrap or a clean kitchen towel
  • Baking sheet or pizza stone
  • Rolling pin (optional)

Instructions for Making Artisan Pizza Dough

Follow these steps to create the perfect artisan pizza dough:

  1. Step 1: Combine the Ingredients

    In a large bowl, mix the flour and all but 1 tablespoon of the water until just combined. Let it rest for 20 minutes. Dissolve the yeast in the remaining warm water.

  2. Step 2: Add Yeast and Salt

    Sprinkle salt over the dough. Pour the yeast mixture over it and begin folding the dough with wet hands to avoid sticking. Repeat this until the dough becomes shaggy.

  3. Step 3: Develop the Gluten

    Fold the dough a few times to build gluten structure. Transfer the dough into a clean, greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 5-6 hours until doubled in size.

  4. Step 4: Shape the Dough

    Once risen, turn the dough onto a floured surface and cut in half. Shape each piece into a ball by folding the edges toward the center. Let rest for an additional hour.

  5. Step 5: Prepare to Bake

    Preheat your oven to 500°F with a pizza stone or steel inside. Flatten each dough ball into a 12-inch disk, keeping a thicker 1-inch lip around the edges.

Tips for Perfect Artisan Pizza Dough

Here are some tips to ensure your artisan pizza dough turns out perfectly every time:

  • Use warm water (not hot) to activate the yeast.
  • Hydration is key to a bubbly crust, so don't be afraid if the dough feel sticky.
  • Resting the dough overnight can enhance its flavor.
